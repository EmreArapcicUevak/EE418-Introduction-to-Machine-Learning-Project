  \section{Introduction}
    \subsection{What is the problem?}
      \begin{frame}{What is the real-world problem?}
        In the modern housing market, both \textbf{apartment sale prices} and \textbf{rental prices} can vary drastically depending
        on factors such as location, size, number of rooms, building condition, and surrounding amenities. \newline \pause

        However, in many listings, especially on local platforms, price information is often missing, incomplete, or inconsistent.
        This makes it difficult for potential buyers, tenants, and even property owners to assess the true value of an apartment or to set a fair and competitive price.
      \end{frame}

    \subsection{What is our solution?}
      \begin{frame}{Our Solution}

        \begin{columns}[T]

          \column{0.45\textwidth}

              \begin{itemize}
                \item[$\bullet$] Predict apartment \textbf{sale} and \textbf{rental} prices
                \item[$\bullet$] Use publicly available real-estate data
                \item[$\bullet$] Provide transparent price estimates
                \item[$\bullet$] Display a ``fairness score`` for listings

  \section{Dataset}
    \subsection{How did we get our data?}
      \begin{frame}{How did we get our data?}
        As of \today~there is yet to be any good publicly available data for apartments that were sold or rented out for Bosnia and Herzegovina. \newline \pause

        The closest we got to finding public data about apartments in Bosnia and Herzegovina was by using the "Registar cijena nekretnina" that can be found
        here (\textit{https://www.katastar.ba/rcn/preglednik/})
      \end{frame}

      \begin{frame}{The problem with the registry}
        So there is a public registry of every apartment sales across the federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. \newline

        So can we use it? \newline \pause

        Short answer\ldots \textbf{no} \newline \pause

        Long answer\ldots The data available on the registry, while plentiful, lacks a lot of details needed for us to train a machine learning model \newline \pause

        This is why, the only available work done on this topic was just a paper demonstrating the price changes and average prices of real estate sales across the region.
      \end{frame}

      \begin{frame}{So how did we get our data?}
        Because of the lack of data available to the public we went with webscraping the data off popular websites for real estate in Bosnia:
        \begin{itemize}
          \item[$\bullet$] OLX.ba
          \item[$\bullet$] Nekretnine.ba
        \end{itemize}

        \vspace{0.5cm}
        \pause
        Out of which we were only able to utilize \emph{OLX.ba} as we lacked the time to further pre-process the data from Nekretnine.ba
      \end{frame}
